## Section A: Short Questions (2 x 5 = 10 marks)

**Q1.** Define *coupling* and *cohesion*. **[2]**
`CO1 | L1 Remember`

**Q2.** What does the CK metric **CBO** measure? **[2]**
`CO2 | L1 Remember`

**Q3.** List four architectural styles. **[2]**
`CO3 | L1 Remember`

**Q4.** State the intent of the **Singleton** pattern. **[2]**
`CO4 | L1 Remember`

**Q5.** What is an **Identity Field** in object-relational mapping? **[2]**
`CO5 | L1 Remember`

---

## Section B: Descriptive Questions (6 x 5 = 30 marks)

**Q6.** Explain *modularity*, *abstraction* and *information hiding*. Why do **low coupling** and **high cohesion** indicate a good split of a system? **[6]**
`CO1 | L2 Understand`

**Q7.** Explain the metrics **LCOM**, **WMC** and **RFC**. What does a value of LCOM2 close to 0 tell you about a class? **[6]**
`CO2 | L2 Understand`

**Q8.** Explain the **Model-View-Controller (MVC)** style and the role of each of its three parts. How does it differ from the **Layered** architectural style? **[6]**
`CO3 | L2 Understand`

**Q9.** Explain the **Strategy** pattern: its intent, its participants (Context, Strategy, ConcreteStrategy), and one benefit and one liability. **[6]**
`CO4 | L2 Understand`

**Q10.** Compare **Single Table Inheritance** and **Class Table Inheritance** for mapping an inheritance hierarchy to a database. Give two advantages and two disadvantages of each. **[6]**
`CO5 | L2 Understand`
